Banana Bread

Soft banana bread for using ripe local bananas before they go to waste.

All Recipes
Total time1 hr 10 min
LevelEasy
Servings1 loaf

Family Note

Let the bananas get spotty. That is when the bread tastes sweetest.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 350 degrees and grease a loaf pan.
  2. Mix mashed bananas, butter, sugar, eggs, and vanilla.
  3. Stir in flour and baking soda just until combined.
  4. Pour into the loaf pan.
  5. Bake 50 to 60 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
