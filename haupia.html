Haupia

A smooth coconut dessert that chills into soft squares for family gatherings.

All Recipes
Total time2 hr 20 min
LevelEasy
Servings16 squares

Family Note

Make this early so it has enough time to chill before cutting.

Ingredients

  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • Pinch of salt

Directions

  1. Whisk coconut milk, water, sugar, cornstarch, and salt in a saucepan.
  2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly.
  3. When the mixture thickens and looks glossy, cook 1 more minute.
  4. Pour into a square pan and smooth the top.
  5. Chill at least 2 hours, then cut into squares.

Cooked with family in mind.
