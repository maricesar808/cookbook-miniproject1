About Grandma Julie

Home-cooked meals made with care

Julie Jeong is a beloved grandmother whose recipes have traveled through family kitchens on paper cards, notebook pages, and memories shared around the table. Born and raised in Hawaii, she brings island warmth, Korean family traditions, and practical home cooking into every meal.

Her specialty is making food that feels comforting and generous: easy to share, full of flavor, and made with the kind of care that makes everyone feel at home.

Designed for family

Big type, simple pages, clear buttons, and no distracting food news. Just the recipes our family wants to find and cook.
