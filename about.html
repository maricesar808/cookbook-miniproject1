About Grandma Julie

Julie Jeong

Julie Jeong is my grandmother, and this website was made to preserve the recipes she has collected, cooked, and shared with our family over the years.

Her Story

Grandma Julie was born and raised in Hawaii, where food is part of family, culture, and everyday connection. Her cooking brings together the comfort of home, the flavors of Hawaii, and family traditions that have been passed down through handwritten recipes and shared meals.

For years, many of her recipes lived on paper. Some were written on cards, some were tucked into notebooks, and some were remembered from cooking together. This website gives those recipes a safer home so our family can keep using them for generations.

What She Cooks Best

Grandma Julie is known for delicious home-cooked meals that feel warm, generous, and easy to share. Her food is practical, comforting, and made with care.

Why this site matters

A recipe box for the whole family

Easy to Use

The site uses large text, simple pages, and clear buttons so Grandma and older family members can navigate it comfortably.

Made for Hawaii

The colors are bright and welcoming, inspired by Hawaii's flowers, ocean, sunshine, and local family kitchens.

Built to Preserve

Digitizing the recipes helps protect them from being lost, damaged, or forgotten as the paper copies get older.

Next Step

Once the family takes photos of each finished meal, those pictures can be added to the recipe cards and individual recipe pages for extra guidance.
