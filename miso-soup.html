Miso Soup

A gentle soup with miso, tofu, seaweed, and green onion for simple family meals.

All Recipes
Total time20 min
LevelEasy
Servings4

Family Note

Do not boil after adding the miso. Keep it gentle.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups dashi or water
  • 3 tablespoons miso paste
  • 1 cup cubed tofu
  • 2 tablespoons dried wakame
  • 2 green onions, sliced

Directions

  1. Warm dashi or water in a pot.
  2. Add tofu and wakame and heat gently.
  3. Scoop some warm broth into a bowl and dissolve miso paste.
  4. Stir dissolved miso back into the pot.
  5. Top with green onion and serve warm.

Cooked with family in mind.
